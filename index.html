NanoWireless Lab · SUNY Polytechnic Institute

Building the wireless links that let tiny devices talk, power up, and heal inside the body.

We design nano-antennas, implantable photonic chips, wireless power systems, and on-device AI, so that future medical devices can sense, report, and act from inside the body.

Our Approach

To work inside the body, a device must communicate, stay powered, interface with living cells, and think for itself. We design all four as one system.

These problems are usually studied by separate research communities. Solving them together is what will make cell-sized implants practical.

01 Communicate Plasmonic nano-antennas and photonic links that carry data to and from single cells. EnablesCell-level sensing and diagnostics 02 Power Battery-free wireless energy delivered safely through tissue, co-designed with the data link. EnablesSmaller, longer-lasting implants 03 Interface Optical and neural interfaces that read from and write to biological systems. EnablesLight-based therapies and neural control 04 Think Small AI models that run on the device itself, so sensors and radios can make decisions without the cloud. EnablesReliable devices that work offline
