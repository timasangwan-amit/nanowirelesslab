Opportunities

Undergraduate Research for SUNY Poly Students

We work with undergraduates on real research problems, and several have become co-authors on papers. We offer independent study credit and RA positions. You do not need prior lab experience. We will teach you what you need. Curiosity about wireless systems, photonics, or medical devices is enough to start.

Open · Rolling

Master's Students in SUNY Poly's Graduate Program

If you are in, or thinking about, SUNY Poly's graduate program and want a thesis or project advisor in nanophotonics, wireless bioelectronics, or communication systems, get in touch. We can work out a thesis topic that fits your background.

Open · By Discussion

Academic Collaborators & Co-Advising

We want to work with faculty at PhD-granting universities, especially in the Northeast, such as RPI, Syracuse, Cornell, Buffalo, and UMass. If your students' work overlaps with ours and you are open to co-advising or joint projects, please get in touch. We bring expertise in nanoscale communication and sensing, and we are happy to share our lab setup.

Open anytime

Industry & Applied Research Partners

SUNY Poly is in the middle of New York's semiconductor and photonics industry. If your company works on nanoscale wireless systems, implant communication, or optical sensing and wants an academic partner, we would be glad to talk.

Open to Discussion

When emailing, use the subject line "[UG Research / MS Thesis / Collaboration]: [Your Name]". We aim to reply within one week.
