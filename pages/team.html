Principal Investigator

Dr. Amit Sangwan

Assistant Professor · College of Engineering, SUNY Polytechnic Institute

Dr. Sangwan founded and directs the NanoWireless Lab at SUNY Polytechnic Institute. His research is on wireless communication and sensing at the nanoscale, including nanophotonic and plasmonic links, wireless power for medical implants, and optical interfaces to cells and neurons.

He believes the next generation of healthcare and connected devices will need wireless links in places today's radio technology cannot reach: inside the body, on a chip, and across a neural interface. He received his PhD from Northeastern University. In his lab, students lead their own projects, write papers as co-authors, and present their work at international conferences.
